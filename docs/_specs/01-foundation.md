# 01 Foundation

## Purpose
Build a personal portfolio for Jeanpaul Garcia that makes a technical co-founder at an early-stage startup stop scanning and want to talk within 60 seconds.

## Primary audience
A product-minded technical co-founder reviewing many candidate tabs quickly on a laptop.

What she needs to understand fast:

- who Jeanpaul is aiming to be
- what he has built
- whether the work is real
- whether he ships with taste and judgment

## Core promise
"I build useful AI tools and ship them."

Everything on the site should make that statement believable within the first screen and strengthen it as the user scrolls.

## Locked brand decisions

### Voice
- Creator-structured, Everyman-voiced
- Plain-spoken first person
- No marketing language
- No inflated claims
- No invented content, claims, numbers, quotes, or achievements
- Exact `[PLACEHOLDER]` tags must be used wherever real values are missing
- Limitations and what does not work must stay visible on every case study page

### Visual direction
- Creator structure with Editorial Luxury layout
- Strict grid underneath the layout
- Calm, restrained, composed, editorial rhythm
- Premium feel through spacing, typography, composition, and real work
- No decorative visual theater

### Proof order
1. Newark Housing Chatbot
2. Study-Guide Generator
3. Voice Consultant

## Non-goals
- No blog
- No newsletter
- No contact form
- No dark mode toggle
- No consent-banner analytics
- No social share buttons
- No chat widget
- No loading screen or hero animation

## Site-level success criteria
- A stranger can identify the target role in five seconds
- A stranger can name at least one specific project after a quick scan
- Each project has proof adjacent to its title and CTA
- The site feels careful and honest, not academic, consultant-like, or oversold
- The codebase reads like a serious junior engineer built it
- Accessibility is treated as a ship blocker, not polish: Lighthouse Accessibility must reach 100 and the site must remain keyboard-usable end to end
- Performance is treated as a ship blocker, not polish: Lighthouse Performance, Best Practices, and SEO must reach at least 95 on production, not just localhost

## Build principle
If a decision is not specified elsewhere, use this filter:

"Will this make Maya stop scrolling and click, or will it slow her down and feel like overselling?"

Choose the faster, cleaner, more believable option.
